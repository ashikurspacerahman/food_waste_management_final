How it works

From a full pan of extra food to a delivered meal — four steps, tracked the whole way.

01

Post surplus food

Donors list what they have, how much, and when it needs to be picked up.

02

Recipients discover it

NGOs and individuals browse nearby listings and request what they can use.

03

A volunteer picks up

Once a request is accepted, a volunteer is assigned to collect and deliver it.

04

Meals get delivered

The donation is marked delivered, and both sides can leave feedback.

Every donation is tracked, start to finish

Sufra records the full lifecycle of a donation — including the ones that don't get claimed in time — so the impact, and the gaps, are visible.

Available

Posted by a donor and open for requests.

Claimed & picked up

A recipient's request was accepted and a volunteer collected it.

Delivered

Food reached the recipient and the donation is complete.

Expired

Nobody claimed it in time — logged as waste, not hidden.
